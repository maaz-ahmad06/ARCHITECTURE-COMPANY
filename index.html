01 / Studio

Honest materials.
Considered light.

We believe a building should tell the truth about how it is made — concrete reads as concrete, timber ages like timber, and light is treated as a material in its own right, not an afterthought.

Every commission starts on site, not on paper. We study the slope of the land, the path of the sun and the grain of the neighbourhood before a single wall is drawn. The result is architecture that feels inevitable, as if it had always belonged where it stands.
